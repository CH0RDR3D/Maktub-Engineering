Solar Energy

Harnessing the Power of the Sun

Zambia receives over 3,000 hours of sunlight per year — one of the richest solar resources in Southern Africa. Maktub Engineering designs, supplies, and installs solar photovoltaic systems for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

  • Residential Solar Systems
    Off-grid and grid-tied rooftop installations for homes — from 2kW starter kits to 20kW+ full-home solutions with battery backup.
  • Commercial & Industrial Solar
    Large-scale solar arrays for factories, warehouses, schools, and government buildings. Significant reduction in operational energy costs.
  • Solar Water Pumping
    Solar-powered borehole and irrigation pumping systems for farms, rural communities, and construction sites across Zambia.

Commitments

Our Environmental Pledges

Sustainability is not a box to tick — it is embedded in how we plan, build, and operate.

Tree Planting Programme
For every major project we complete, Maktub Engineering plants trees in partnership with local communities. Our target is 5,000 trees by 2027 — restoring natural canopy, preventing erosion, and offsetting carbon across our project footprints in Lusaka, Muchinga, and beyond.
Solar-First Site Policy
All Maktub Engineering site offices and contractor camps are powered by solar energy where grid connection is unavailable or unreliable. This policy eliminates diesel generator dependency on site — reducing costs, noise, and emissions for the duration of every project.
Water Conservation
Rainwater harvesting and water-recycling systems are incorporated into new building designs wherever feasible. On construction sites, dust suppression uses recycled water, and all wastewater is managed in compliance with ZEMA's Environmental Management Act regulations.
Responsible Procurement
We evaluate all suppliers on environmental criteria — prioritising locally sourced, sustainably produced materials. Timber is sourced only from certified sustainable forestry operations, and we actively seek low-embodied-carbon alternatives to conventional building materials.
