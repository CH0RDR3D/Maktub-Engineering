Building Construction
Quality residential, commercial, and institutional construction. We handle every phase from foundation to finishing with precision, safety, and timely delivery.
Maktub is Grade 5 NCC certified. Our expertise extends to complex structural projects, high-end residential finishes, and government-standard facilities. We handle the full project lifecycle: architectural review, site preparation, structural framing, and specialized installations including plumbing, electrical, and HVAC. We guarantee strict adherence to the Zambian Building Code and environmental safety standards.
Road & Bridge Works
Professional road and bridge construction including earthworks and drainage. NCC registered (Grade 5 Category C) valid through December 2026.
As a Category C registered contractor, we deliver urban asphalt surfacing, feeder road maintenance, and structural bridge engineering. Our capabilities include massive earthworks, complex drainage culverts, and high-load reinforced concrete structures. We focus on durability, ensuring infrastructure can withstand Zambia's seasonal weather changes and heavy logistical transport requirements.
Maintenance & Renovation
Expert repair and refurbishment services to restore functionality and extend the service life of aging buildings and infrastructure.
We provide turnkey refurbishment for commercial and institutional assets. Our services include structural integrity audits, specialized roof waterproofing, industrial electrical upgrades, and modern interior redesigns. We help organizations modernize their footprint while significantly extending the operational life and market value of their properties.
Equipment Supplies
Reliable construction and engineering equipment sourced for quality and durability. Supporting complex projects with machinery that meets operational demands.
We leverage a global supply chain to provide high-performance machinery, including CAT earthmovers, high-capacity concrete mixers, and industrial-grade generators. Our procurement process includes quality verification, duty clearance, and specialized logistics to deliver equipment directly to active sites in even the most remote areas of Zambia.
Repair & Servicing
Professional repair and maintenance to keep machinery running at peak efficiency, minimizing downtime and keeping your projects on schedule.
Minimize downtime with our specialized repair team. We offer expert hydraulic system rebuilding, diesel engine overhauls, and preventative maintenance schedules for construction fleets. Our mobile technicians provide on-site support to ensure your equipment remains productive and safe throughout the project duration.
Spare Parts Supply
Genuine spare parts for construction equipment. Authentic components that maintain performance and extend equipment lifespan.
We maintain an extensive inventory of genuine OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) components for brands like Komatsu, JCB, and Volvo. From high-pressure seals and heavy-duty filters to engine internal components and specialized wear parts, we ensure your machinery retains its factory-standard performance and safety ratings.
