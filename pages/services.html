What We Do

Our Services

From civil engineering and road construction to office furniture and PPE — we cover it all under one roof.

Building Construction
Construction of residential, commercial, and institutional buildings with a focus on quality, safety, and timely delivery. We handle every phase from foundation to finishing, ensuring structures that stand the test of time.
Road & Bridge Construction
Execution of road and bridge works including earthworks, drainage, and structural construction in line with engineering standards. We are NCC registered (Grade 5 Category C — General Civil Engineering Works) valid through December 2026.
Maintenance & Renovation
Repair, refurbishment, and maintenance of buildings and infrastructure to improve functionality and extend service life. We bring ageing structures back to full operational capacity.
Equipment Supplies
Supply of reliable construction and engineering equipment for various project requirements. We source quality machinery that meets the operational demands of even the most complex projects.
Equipment Repair & Servicing
Repair and servicing of construction and engineering equipment to ensure operational efficiency and minimise downtime. Keeping your machinery running means your projects stay on schedule.
Spare Parts Supply
Provision of genuine spare parts for construction and engineering machinery to support uninterrupted project operations. We supply authentic components that extend equipment life and maintain performance.
Office Equipment
Supply of office equipment and furniture to support efficient workplace operations. From printers and copiers to ergonomic workstation setups — we equip offices to perform at their best.
Stationery & Consumables
Provision of essential office stationery and consumables. We supply everything from pens and paper to toner and filing systems, ensuring your team has what it needs.
Office & School Furniture
Provision of durable office and school furniture including desks, chairs, cabinets, and storage solutions. We serve both corporate offices and educational institutions across Zambia.
Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Supply of safety equipment to support safe working environments. From hard hats and high-visibility vests to gloves and safety boots — protecting your workforce is our priority.
Construction Materials
Supply of essential construction materials including cement, aggregates, steel, and related building inputs. We source quality materials to keep your projects moving without delays.
General Hardware
Supply of tools, fittings, and general hardware for construction and maintenance works. We stock a comprehensive range of hardware to support any job on site or in the workshop.
